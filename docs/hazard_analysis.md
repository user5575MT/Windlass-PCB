# Hazard analysis

Design-stage analysis for the board described in `architecture.md`. It is not a substitute for a sea trial, and it is not a claim that the interlock has been built or tested. Controls that depend on unmeasured wiring are marked OPEN.

Severity is about the windlass and the people near the chain, not about the PCB as a product.

| ID | Hazard | How this board could cause it | Mitigation intended on this PCB | Residual | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Both directions energised | Both GPIOs asserted, firmware crash, or a driver fault | XOR plus cross-clamps, and the series FET also requires a fresh heartbeat. Switch and ignition are in the coil path | A welded contact is not opened by coil exclusion. See H2 | Drawn in rev 0.2. Not bench tested. Relay current still OPEN |
| H2 | Contact welded or stuck closed | Contactor current above the contact rating; no suppression; contact simply fails | Coil exclusion and the series FET do not open a welded contact. The shared +12 V node cannot distinguish a weld from the hand remote or the Lofrans remote, so this revision has no feedback input that claims to detect a weld | A welded contact runs until the breaker or the existing feed is removed. Relay MPN is OPEN until inrush and hold are known | OPEN. Detection is not claimed |
| H3 | GPIO stuck in the ON drive level | Firmware hang while the pin is already driven, or a short | 74LVC1G123 drops `Q_SERIES` about 330 ms after the last rising edge on GPIO1. A static high does not refresh it. Direction pulldowns cover reset | One stuck direction still runs while heartbeats continue. The current firmware does not toggle GPIO1, so the coils stay off until a new image is reviewed | Drawn. The kick itself is not in the prototype firmware, on purpose |
| H4 | Command link lost, windlass keeps running | Wireless hold not refreshed | Existing firmware TTL 300 ms writes the direction requests off. That TTL is unchanged. The hardware timer is a separate backstop if edges on GPIO1 also stop | The TTL does not run if the CPU is halted. The hardware timer covers that only once a future image is actually toggling GPIO1 | Software contract exists. Hardware timer is drawn. Not tested |
| H5 | Brownout or reboot glitch pulses a coil | Pin state during reset, 3.3 V collapsing slower than coil supply | Direction requests and GPIO1 are pulled down. Monostable Q is low until a rising edge. Loss of 3.3 V releases the series FET through its gate pulldown | Not a scope capture. One glitch on GPIO1 can open a single timeout window, and the direction pulldowns still have to hold | Drawn. Not measured |
| H6 | Backfeed into the hand remote or Lofrans box | Parallel contacts share a feed the other controller did not expect | Series orientation and suppression taken from B1. No path from our coil supply onto the sense lines | Unknown until B1 is measured | OPEN. Blocks the output sheet |
| H7 | Reverse battery or load dump damages the board and faults the coil drivers on | 12 V input unprotected, or TVS and fuse uncoordinated | MCU branch: 2 A fuse, DMP6023LE, SMAJ18A (clamp about 29.2 V). Coil branch is its own 1 A fuse, DMP6023LE, and SMAJ18A, sized for the 42 mA Finder coil, so coil current does not flow through the MCU FET | SMAJ18A’s 400 W 10/1000 µs rating is not a load-dump approval on either branch. Source impedance is B6 | Coil parts selected. Load-dump energy OPEN |
| H8 | Lofrans reed or counter misbehaves | Input current too high (already observed: 3.22 V to 1.5 V) | ISO7721 at 3.3 V, 10 kΩ series, 47 kΩ pull-down sized for ±10 µA leakage. Dry-contact pull-up is switched away from the Lofrans pin | Source impedance is still unmeasured. Idle must be checked on the boat | Calculated. Boat check OPEN |
| H9 | Isolation bridged; NMEA ground tied to windlass 12 V | NET-S connected to local 12 V, shield used as ground, or isolator creepage violated | ISO1042BDWR and two MEJ2 modules. Secondaries not joined. NET-S has no copper. Continuous barrier claim is the MEJ2’s 2.4 kVDC functional rating, not the 5 kVrms hipot | A field wiring error can still bridge it. PCB creepage is not laid out. Murata does not class MEJ2 as a safety barrier, and ships need their written approval, which is not claimed | Parts cited. Creepage OPEN until layout |
| H10 | Two chain counts on the backbone | This board and the Waveshare both transmit 128777 | Publish-enable strap holds the CAN driver off in the wireless-RX position. Only one position may transmit | Someone can set the strap on and leave the Waveshare connected | Operational control required. B8 |
| H11 | Opposite commands from this board and the Lofrans remote | PCB interlock does not see the other controller’s contacts | None fully effective on this PCB. Existing controls are required to keep working with this board removed, so this board must not sit in series with them | Both contactors can be asked to run. Mitigation is procedural and, if wanted later, a system interlock outside this PCB that still fails safe when this board is removed | Accepted as residual unless the owner adds a system-level device. Needs an explicit answer |
| H12 | Software bypasses ignition or the relay switch | Enable implemented as a GPIO | Those two devices are in the coil current path. GPIO can only request a coil that already has that feed | A person can still close the switch. That is the point of the switch | Topology fixed in architecture. Implementation OPEN |
| H13 | Operator trusts the LED | Green read as “safe to work on the windlass” | LED is not in the control path. Documentation and silk must not say SAFE. Amber means only that this board’s coils are unpowered | The Lofrans remote can still run the windlass with this LED amber | Permanent residual |
| H14 | Loss of radio in a metal box, stale commands | Antenna against metal, keepout violated | 15 mm clearance and edge placement, or do not ship that enclosure | A lost radio must drop coils via the TTL. It must not be patched by holding the last command | Enclosure OPEN (B6) |

## Review gate

Rev 0.2 draws the interlock, the heartbeat, and the split 12 V rails. Layout is still not started. An independent read of H1, H2, H3, and H11, and of `design_review.md`, is the gate before any routing. B1, B2, B3, and B6 are still open measurements. B7’s timer is drawn; the firmware kick is deliberately not added to the prototype.
